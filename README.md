# MSc Analytics Reports: Summaries

Short summaries of three written reports from my MSc Business Analytics at the University of Greenwich (2025–26). Each covers the question, the approach, the main recommendations and the limitations. The analysis projects with code are in separate repositories, linked from my [GitHub profile](https://github.com/rahat28ahmed).

The full reports are not published here. They are available on request.

| Report | Module | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| [Ethics and Accountability in Algorithmic Decision-Making](summaries/ethics-algorithmic-accountability.md) | Ethical and Legal Aspects of Business Analytics | Transparency and accountability in COMPAS, the 2018 Uber self-driving fatality and Clearview AI, judged against ethical theory and EU, UK and US law |
| [Agentic AI for Promotional Stock Replenishment](summaries/agentic-ai-lunex-uk.md) | Digital Economy, Tools and Techniques | A governed agentic AI system to close a three-hour restocking gap at a 320-store UK grocer (case study) |
| [Blockchain for Verified Emissions at DHL](summaries/blockchain-emissions-dhl.md) | Digital Economy, Tools and Techniques | A permissioned blockchain to make DHL's emissions and carbon-offset data verifiable across partners |
